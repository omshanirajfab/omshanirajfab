About Us

Fabrication work, built on engineering discipline.

Om Shanirajeshwar Fabrication is an industrial fabrication company based in Chikhali, PCMC, Pune. We take on the structural work that keeps a factory running — sheds and building structures, and the crane hoist systems that move heavy loads across a shop floor.

Our work sits at the point where design meets steel. Before we cut a single section, we work out what the structure actually needs to carry: the span of a shed roof, the travel of a hoist, the loading of a runway beam. That thinking is what separates a structure that lasts from one that doesn't.

We work directly with factory owners, contractors, and site engineers — visiting the site, understanding the constraints, and fabricating to match. Every job, whatever the size, gets the same attention to fit, finish, and load.

Our Approach

What guides every job.

  • Understand the load first

    Every structure is sized to what it will actually carry, not a standard template.

  • Fabricate for the site

    Sections are cut and welded to fit real site conditions, reducing rework during erection.

  • Stay accountable

    We remain the point of contact from quotation through installation and after.

  • Build for daily use

    Sheds and hoist structures are built to withstand continuous industrial operation.

Fabrication & Erection Capability

From Raw Steel to Commissioned Factory

PHASE 1: WORKSHOP FABRICATION

In-House Fabrication & QA

Heavy steel section cutting, base plate welding, gusset stiffening, and dimensional inspection before dispatch.

PHASE 2: GRID ERECTION

Foundation Anchoring & Plumb

Precision column erection on reinforced footings with rigorous laser and plumb-line alignment across the site grid.

PHASE 3: TRUSS & CRANE HOIST

On-Site Assembly & Hoisting

Ground-level triangular truss assembly followed by synchronized mobile crane hoisting for safe structural integration.
