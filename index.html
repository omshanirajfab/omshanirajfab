Why OSF

Work that's engineered first, fabricated second.

Every job starts with the load it has to carry — the weight of a hoist, the span of a shed, the demands of a factory floor. We plan around that before a single piece of steel is cut, so what goes up stays sound for years of daily use.

Based in Chikhali, PCMC, we work directly with factory owners, contractors, and site engineers across Pune to deliver structures that are on-spec, on-site, and on time.

  • Engineered to load

    Structures sized and detailed for the actual weight and use they'll see, not guesswork.

  • Site-ready fabrication

    Cut, welded, and finished for straightforward erection with minimal site rework.

  • Local, hands-on service

    Based in PCMC — close enough to visit site, measure, and follow through in person.

  • Crane & hoist expertise

    Specialised experience in the structures that carry moving industrial loads.
