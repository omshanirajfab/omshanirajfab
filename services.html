What We Do

Services built around industrial steel.

01 — Fabrication Work

Structural steel fabrication

We cut, weld, and assemble structural steel to drawing — columns, beams, trusses, platforms, and custom industrial components. Every piece is fabricated to fit, checked for alignment, and finished ready for site erection.

  • Steel cutting, welding & assembly
  • Custom brackets, platforms & walkways
  • Structural repair & reinforcement
  • Fabrication to client drawing or site measurement
02 — Sheds & Structures

Industrial sheds & factory structures

From a single-bay shed to a full factory frame, we design and build structures sized to your plot and your production layout — steel columns, roof trusses, purlins, and cladding support, built for long-term industrial use.

  • Pre-engineered shed structures
  • Factory & warehouse building frames
  • Roof trusses, purlins & column structures
  • Site erection & installation
03 — Crane Hoist Systems

EOT crane & hoist structures

We fabricate and install the girders, runway beams, and support structures that carry EOT cranes and hoist systems — engineered for the load and travel your material handling actually needs.

  • Crane girders & runway beams
  • Hoist support structures
  • Column brackets & crane rails
  • Installation & site alignment
04 — Roofing & Skylights

Roof trusses & daylight systems

Precision truss fabrication and daylight roofing solutions that maximize natural light, reduce factory energy costs, and ensure superior rainwater drainage and ventilation.

  • High-span roof truss design & fabrication
  • Polycarbonate daylight skylight panels
  • Purlins, gutters & down-take systems
  • Color-coated profile roof sheeting
